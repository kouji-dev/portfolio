Privacy

Short version: this site counts visits with Google Analytics. That’s all it collects. No accounts, no forms, no newsletter.

Visit counting

I use Google Analytics to see how many people visit and which parts they look at, like a project link or a CV download. To do that, Google puts a small cookie in your browser and sees things like your browser, rough location and the pages you open. It doesn’t know your name.

Don’t want that? Any ad blocker stops it, and the site works exactly the same.

The painting

The animated painting remembers how smoothly your device can run it, so it doesn’t have to test again. That note stays in your browser and is deleted when you close the tab.

Other services

The site runs on Vercel, and the 3D painting loads one library from jsDelivr. Like any website, they see your IP address when your browser asks them for files. Links to GitHub, LinkedIn and others follow those sites’ own rules.
