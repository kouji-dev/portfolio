Privacy

This site doesn’t store any of your data. It only counts visits, and that’s it.

What’s counted

Google Analytics tells me how many people visit and which pages or links they open, like a project or a CV download. It’s anonymous: no name, no email, nothing that says who you are.

What isn’t

No accounts, no forms, no newsletter. I don’t keep any data about you.

Rather not be counted?

Any ad blocker stops it. The site works exactly the same.
