Privacy

This is the personal site of Driss Najih, a freelance software engineer in Paris. It has no accounts, no forms and no newsletter, so the only data it handles is what is described below.

Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 (property G-YJ4W6QWCMZ) to count visits and see which sections, projects and CV downloads people use. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (_ga, _ga_*) and receives your IP address, browser, device type, approximate location, referring page and the pages and links you click. The script loads only after the page has finished loading. Google processes this data as described in its privacy policy. You can block it with any content blocker or the Google Analytics opt-out add-on; the site works the same without it.

Stored in your browser

The painting on the home page remembers the animation quality your device can handle in sessionStorage for the current tab. It never leaves your browser and is cleared when the tab closes.

Hosting and third parties

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs. Fonts are self-hosted. The 3D painting loads three.js from the jsDelivr CDN, which sees your IP address like any web server. Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, X, Google Calendar and Figma take you to those services and their own policies.

Your rights and contact

Under the GDPR you can ask what data concerns you, or have it deleted. The analytics data is pseudonymous and held by Google; write to najih.driss73@gmail.com with any question and I will answer within 30 days.
